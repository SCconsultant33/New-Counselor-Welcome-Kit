01

Welcome

You have a countywide counseling community behind you.

Whether you are brand new to school counseling or starting in a new district, I hope you come to feel at home here in Oakland County.

Oakland Schools serves all 28 traditional school districts and many public school academies in the county. Across that community, approximately 320 school counselors are doing thoughtful work for students and families. This guide is designed to help you find the people, communication channels, learning, and resources available to you.

I hope you will connect beyond your building, take advantage of countywide opportunities, and reach out whenever I can support your school counseling efforts.

— Sean

About Sean

Meet your county guide

Sean Dougherty · School Counseling Consultant · Oakland Schools

Sean has served as the School Counseling Consultant at Oakland Schools since 2019. Before that, he spent 11 years as a school counselor at Lakeland High School, where he served as department chair and district cohort leader for counselors in Huron Valley Schools.

He earned a bachelor's degree in Psychology from the University of Michigan and a master's degree in Counseling with a School Counselor specialization from Oakland University. He is a Licensed Professional Counselor, National Certified Counselor, and licensed school counselor.

Sean also taught a graduate-level school counseling course at Eastern Michigan University and serves on the Oakland Counseling Association board.

04

School Counseling Consultant Services

Support for the work in front of you

Consultation, coaching, resource development, and learning can be shaped around the needs of individual counselors, teams, and districts.

Coaching support

Purposeful conversations for reflection and problem-solving

New-counselor coaching can help counselors in their first years build confidence, strengthen professional skills, and navigate current challenges. Counselor-leadership coaching supports program leaders with data-based decisions, educational systems, and meaningful change.

Conversations may be virtual or in person, typically last 20–30 minutes, and can occur monthly or as needed.
